Finder tags

Control-1 to Control-7 add a tag to the image that is shown; pressing the key again removes the tag. The tags are named and colored as in the Finder's settings (renamed tags keep their new name), and the image's other tags are kept. The tags are also in File > Tags, which shows the tags the image has. The info bubble (I) shows the image's colored tags as dots after its name; when the bubble is hidden, it appears for a moment when a tag changes.

Only images that are files on disk can be tagged, not images in archives or PDF pages. If Control-1 to Control-7 switch to another desktop instead, turn off Switch to Desktop in System Settings > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts > Mission Control.

Menu commands without a shortcut

View > Transition (the page transition and its speed), View > Folder Depth (how many levels of subfolders are included), View > Background (Ambient, Black or the Settings color). The Finder's Services menu and Quick Actions have Open in Sequential for folders, PDFs and archives.
